ABOUT US — THE ARENA

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena was established in 2023 by Jason Naidoo, with a simple goal - to create a safe, modern and exciting experience, where everyone can play, compete, space where gamers and communities can come together.

What started as a vision has grown into a fully equipped arena offering gaming, esports tournaments, birthday parties, Esports tournaments school outings and corporate team-building experiences.

MISSION

Our mission is to provide fun, accessible and high-quality gaming experiences for individuals, groups, schools and businesses.

VISION

Our vision is to become a leading gaming and esports destination where people can connect, compete and experience the next level of gaming.

GOALS

Our goals are to provide modern gaming technologies, bring gamers and communities together and to create memorable gaming experiences.
